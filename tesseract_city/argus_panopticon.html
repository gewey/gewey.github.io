Why Fake "AI Machine Code" (#2 & #8) Loses to Telegraphic English (#3)

Modern BPE tokenizers (o200k_base / cl100k_base) are trained predominantly on natural English prose where a leading space + whole English word is a single token (" read", " not", " all", " full" = 1 token each).

When an LLM tries to "compress" English by gluing words together with snake_case, ::, =>, output.lines[*], or Unicode math symbols (¬, ∧, ≠, ≫), it destroys leading-space word merges and inflates token count from 127 tokens (#1) up to 149–162 tokens (#2 & #8)!
