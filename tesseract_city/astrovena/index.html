Project Tesseract: Executive Summary

Project Tesseract is an action-RPG set inside a cybernetic grid matrix. Players control rogue AI agents navigating procedurally reconfiguring sector towers.

Spec Key Value
Genre Isometric Cyberpunk Action-RPG
Target Engine Three.js WebGL / Custom C++ Engine
Target Platform Web Browser (PWA) / Steam Desktop
Target Framerate 60 FPS @ 1080p (Web), 144 FPS (Desktop)

Core Gameplay Loop & Systems

Players infiltrate sectors, extract memory cores, upgrade agent neural chips, and defend against rogue security subroutines.

Step Section Est Time Result
1 Foundation (World Overview) 15 - 20 minutes A one-sentence concept, tone, genre, and what makes this world different
2 Locations 20 - 30 minutes 3-5 key places your story visits
3 Magic / Power System 15 - 25 minutes The rules of what’s possible in your world
4 Culture & Factions 25 - 35 minutes Who you world’s people are, and who holds power over them
5 Plot Connections 10 - 15 minutes How this world specifically challenges and shapes your protagonist/characters

Total time : 85 - 125 minutes (about 2 hrs)
