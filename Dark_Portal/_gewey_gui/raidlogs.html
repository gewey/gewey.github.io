The Tenure Rule

Only tenure-era guild history belongs to Tandu. <Gehenna> counts during the BfA era (~2018–2019) — the window Joey's own 2019 bookmark sheet pins down: Warcraft Logs guild 7287, Wipefest, and the WoWProgress Shadowsong / Borean Tundra connected-cluster pages, Uldir & Battle of Dazar'alor season. <Theorycrafters> on Thrall / Hyjal follows after. Pre-tenure guild snapshots (the WotLK–Legion Wayback captures of Gehenna) are the guild's history, not the character's — they are never indexed as his.

This pass still walked all 23 archived guild pages — as a negative-control sweep: if Tandu had appeared in any pre-tenure roster, the tenure rule itself would need a second look. It did not appear anywhere (next section), which is exactly what the rule predicts.

What the Macro Cache Remembers

With no 2015-era combat logs on disk, the account-wide macros-cache.txt is the closest thing to a raid diary the retail tree keeps — each macro block is an era fingerprint.

Shadowlands era (2020–2022)

  • The covenant-detection hunter macro: SetMacroSpell(... G"Resonating Arrow" or G"Flayed Shot" or G"Death Chakram" or G"Wild Spirits") — one macro that recolors itself for Kyrian / Necrolord / Venthyr / Night Fae.
  • /btwl a Tandu — a BtWLoadouts assignment that still points at the 2008 handle.

The utility era

  • The !!-prefixed block: bars toggler, then the classic max-graphics clamp (groundEffectDensity 256, farclip 777, maxLOD 3, SkyCloudLOD 3).
  • !!Abandon Quests, !!Melee template, boss-1 focus loop with Counter shot, Tranquilizing Shot castsequence.

The character cache on Tandumonium/Hyjal is the Midnight kit (Barbed/Cobra/Steady/Bursting spec conditionals, Wing Buffet racial on the bar, @cursor binding shot). Its file stamp — 2026-08-14 14:28 — is the oldest file on the whole retail tree. That date is the bottom of the local record; everything older exists only on the internet, which is why the tenure-era hunt above exists.
