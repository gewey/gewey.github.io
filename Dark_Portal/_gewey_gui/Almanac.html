what this wing adds
Deeds counts what the biography database wrote down. The Almanac reads the metering addons instead: ForeverStats keeps a rung-by-rung ledger of every level (kills, quests, XP source, coin in and out, yards walked), FamiliarFaces keeps every NPC and player it ever hovered, Goldmind keeps the auction-house ledger and the loot tape, and Overlord keeps the war-front scoreboard. None of these files had a page before today.

05. Method & Limitshow the numbers were taken, and what they refuse

what the residue says
  • The Barrens is the busiest face-zone: 559 encounters logged.
  • Tandumonium is the largest single purse, holding 1g 38s 62c.
  • The war map is split 34 Alliance / 33 Horde across 67 fronts.
  • 276,982 yards were walked, ridden and flown.
  • 30 meter sessions were recorded, each keeping 9 meters.
how it was read
Every SavedVariables file is tokenised once, then only the top-level keys this wing needs are materialised, so a 4 MB Goldmind.lua indexes in about a second. Timestamps are the addons' own epoch seconds, rendered in local time. Where an item ID has a cached tooltip in Goldmind's items table the name is resolved; otherwise the raw ID is printed rather than guessed.
what it does not claim
FamiliarFaces only records units the cursor touched or the nameplate exposed, so 1,528 is a floor, not a census. PlayerHistory is retail-era residue keyed by realm, not a Forever roster. Overlord's fronts are the addon's synthetic campaign, not live realm state. Gold is copper as cached by the addon and may lag the last sale.
