Three skins, one spine

I. Blood Elf 2008 →

The true form, and the one every other form is a costume over. Sin'dorei: the survivors who fed on something they were not supposed to survive. It is not a phase of the account — it is the shape the account keeps returning to.

What the body gave me: Arcane Torrent — a purge of magic in an area and a resource refill. Fitting for a race whose whole history is "we ate the wrong thing and kept going."

Every visage below this one is a loan.

II. Undead Forsaken stint

The grave-dirt years. The body stopped being warm; the character did not stop being a blood elf. This is the only skin on the list I did not choose for a reason I can defend mechanically — it was the era, it was the faction, it was what the account was doing.

What the body gave me: Will of the Forsaken — fear, sleep and charm break on command. The one mechanical gift that matters when the raid is stacked against you.

Undeath is a lease, not a lineage.

III. Dracthyr 2026 main

Chosen, deliberately, for one reason: Glide. Gravity has killed this character more times than any boss ever did, and the dracthyr is the only body on the list that argues back when it falls. Everything else about the race was a bonus.

What the body gave me: Glide — and a visage, which I set to blood elf, because of course I did.

A parachute with a personality.

The wider account proves the pattern is not vanity: nine living characters answer on the armory across six races — dracthyr, undead, haranir, Zandalari troll, worgen, void elf — with the blood-elf spine underneath every one of them. Census and per-character evidence: Dynasty.

Two lives, one name

Since 2026 the same character is being played twice: once in the retail timeline that started in 2008, and once in a Forever realm where the client itself is new and the character carries the old name into it. They do not share a server, a save file, or a single byte of data. They share the name, the specs I like, and the habit of uploading my own logs.
