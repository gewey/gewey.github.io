The Witnesses — what the databases say

A character is easy to lie about. A database is not. The armory forgot the handle in 2020; the addon on the disk still has it. Raider.IO’s client cache remembered the five people I last looked up, and its US tables carry a million characters’ worth of names that are not me. This page is the account’s case file: five witnesses, one collision register, one closed census, and two nights the client fell over.

Three rules, then a witness

The tenure rule

A guild is only mine during the era I was inside it. <Gehenna> 2019, yes. <Gehenna> 2009–2016 Wayback captures, never.

The name rule

A Tand-shaped name on a realm I never played is a stranger. Tandemonium lives on Argent Dawn. That is not this account.

The realm rule

Shadowsong, Borean Tundra, Thrall, Hyjal. Four retail realms, two clusters. Anything outside them needs an owner field.

All three rules read the same way: positive evidence beats a resemblance. What follows is positive evidence only.

5. The census that closed

Between 2005 and 2016 the only public record of a levelling character was warcraftrealms.com — a crowd-sourced census with first-seen and last-seen dates per name. For a 2008 blood elf it was the closest thing to a birth certificate.

Asked today, that domain serves a Midnight item database titled “Midnight Database — Warcraft DB”. The census is gone from the surface web; the archive answers on its behalf. Verified this run: archive.org/wayback/available?url=warcraftrealms.com/charsheet.php returns available: true with a closest snapshot, so the shape of the census survives even where the rows do not.

Practically: the pre-2018 decade has no machine-readable witness left. Everything the Origins wing asserts for 2008–2016 is reconstruction from macro text, ghost addons and memory — not from a census row. That is the honest ceiling, and it is written down here instead of assumed.

The character renamed on 2020-09-16 — the rosters prove it, “Tandu” stops and “Tandieu” begins. The logs do not notice. All 470 reports, from the first Uldir night in October 2018 to the last pull in December 2024, carry the same owner value: Tandu. Warcraft Logs stores the account, not the character sheet.

That is the cleanest proof of identity in the whole file: three handles, four realms, two installs, one owner. The name changed; the person filing the paperwork did not.
