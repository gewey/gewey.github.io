Method & limits

A cache is not a history. A record exists because the client needed it, not because Tandumonium earned it; the WDB keeps no timestamps, so no date can be attached to any name on this page. The three stills are the only items here with a real clock.

Read-only probes: Research/_local/_souvenirs_probe.py (headers, meter strings, log and screenshot inventory) and Research/_local/_wdb_parse.py (record walker: signature, build, then repeated [id][size][payload]). Neither writes into the game tree. Related wings: Regalia (the armory, read raw), Witnesses (third-party memory incl. the crash dumps), Strata (per-shell CVar tape), Watchtower (logs and boot tape).
