08. Method & Limitswhat the ladder measures and what it omits

what the levelbook says
  • Fastest completed rung: level 22 in 17m 53s; longest: level 20 in 4h 52m.
  • Deadliest rung: level 9 with 3 deaths.
  • 167,369 of the 276,982 yards were on foot; 102,134 were flown.
  • 36 gear changes and 16 recipes were recorded.
how it was read
ForeverStats keeps levels[] keyed by rung, each carrying time, kills, quests, deaths, XP by source, coin in/out, distance and activity. Milestones are the addon's own dated events: zone, area, level, skill and spell. Meter sessions are the last 30 recorded fights; the nine meters are DamageDone, HealingDone, Absorbs, DamageTaken, AvoidableDamageTaken, EnemyDamageTaken, Interrupts, Dispels and Deaths.
what it does not claim
Time is the addon's online clock, so a session that crashed is short by the missing minutes. Money is copper recorded at the moment of the transaction, not a bank statement. XP totals omit rest bonus. Perf maxMs of 2.58 ms is the addon's own worst frame while metering, not the client's.
