Origins the ten years the archive never saw

Every other wing of this HUD begins where the evidence begins: four hundred and seventy Warcraft Logs reports, the first of them on 2018-10-01. The character is ten years older than that first upload. This page is the negative space — what can be proven about 2008–2018, what can only be attested, and what the archive's silence is actually made of.

I did not start existing when I started uploading. I started uploading when someone else built a website that would keep the numbers.

Attested — what only the player can say

Some of the record has no file behind it and never will. It is not weaker evidence, it is a different kind: a statement by the only witness. It is labelled attested everywhere it appears, so no future pass can mistake it for a crawl.

2008 · Shadowsong attested

The account begins in 2008 on Shadowsong-US as a blood elf. Not a transfer, not a reroll with a new name: the same handle, the same realm cluster, the same faction, for eighteen years.

That realm cluster still holds 12 character folders today — the largest retail block on disk after Hyjal.

Tandu · the OG handle attested

Short, blunt, two syllables. Everything later — Tandieu, Tandumonium, Tandumonium-Ebbeson — is an elaboration of it, usually forced by a name collision on a transfer.

Four spellings, one spine: Legacy · The Four Handles.

The bodies attested

Blood elf throughout, with one detour through undeath and one deliberate respec into dracthyr — chosen for Glide, because gravity has killed this character more often than any boss.

Persona carries the long version; this card exists so Origins is not read as a race history.

Why attestation is fenced off. A pre-2018 armory page would be evidence; a memory of 2008 is testimony. Mixing the two is how a legacy page starts inventing guilds. This wing keeps them in separate columns, and the tenure rule keeps every pre-2019 <Gehenna> capture out of both.

Orphans — what outlived its addon

The retail install cannot say what happened in 2011. It can say what was installed and then deleted, because deleting an addon folder does not delete the things that pointed at it. Those pointers are the closest thing to a fossil this decade has.

WorldFlightMap enable lists on 51 of 54 characters A flight-path overlay the retail AGENTS.md calls "removed since the 14-addon era". Two sources, one absence.
!Swatter enable lists on 54 of 54 characters The error-swallowing engine under !BugGrabber. Every character still thinks it is loaded.
!ImprovedErrorFrame enable lists on 54 of 54 characters Swatter's older sibling. Same story: a name with no code behind it.

Installed today: 40 addon folders. Account macro cache: 98 macros across 489 lines, 42 of them carrying a /click into some bar that may or may not still exist. That ratio — one live folder to two remembered names — is the decade in miniature.

An addon leaves nothing behind but the fact that you once needed it. That is also the best description of the years 2008 to 2018.

31 of 33 doors answered with nothing. The two that answered hold six captures between them, and every one of those six is a capture of a guild page, not of me: a guild name resolving on a date proves the name existed, never that I was in it. They are counted here and linked nowhere.

A control URL was run in the same session — a well-known character on a full-population realm, plus its WoWProgress sheet — and both returned zero as well. The emptiness is a property of how the archive crawled character sheets, not a property of these handles: individual armory pages were rendered client-side for most of the 2008–2016 window, which is exactly the kind of page a crawler saves as a shell and forgets.

Tenure rule applied to the six: the four 2016 <Theorycrafters> captures on Hyjal predate any membership this record can establish, and the two Raider.IO captures are name-level only. None of the six is quoted, dated into a timeline, or presented as tenure — the same rule that keeps 24 pre-tenure <Gehenna> captures out of Legacy.

What the silence is worth

Ten years is not a hole in the story, it is most of the story: the levelling, the first raid, the first guild that did not work out, the undeath detour, the transfers, the name that had to change because someone on the destination realm already had it. None of that produced an upload, because nothing was uploading yet.

What survived instead is the shape of the habits: a Decursive macro that refuses to die, dialog keys for a quest mod that was uninstalled, a bind that clicks a bar belonging to an addon that is not installed, 54 character folders across three realms where 9 of them were ever played. Habits leave sediment. Achievements leave rows. Only one of the two is archivable, and it is not the one that made me.

Tenure rule, restated because this is the page most likely to break it

The Wayback index holds 26 captures of <Gehenna> on Borean Tundra. Exactly two fall inside the 2019 BfA membership — 2019-08-19 and 2020-08-07 — and those are already recorded on Legacy. The other 24 predate the guild and are never linked, quoted or counted, here or in any other wing, page or agent file. <Theorycrafters> is recorded only as the guild the account moved to, on Thrall-US and Hyjal-US.

Method: read-only HTTP GET against the Wayback CDX API, one browser user agent, no cookies, no authentication, exact-URL queries with collapse=digest, throttled against the archive's own 429s. The raw payload lives in Research\_raw\legacy_cdx.json and is gitignored because no page reads it at runtime — every figure above is baked in at build time.
