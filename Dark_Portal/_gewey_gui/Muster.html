Muster the roll call behind the logs

Four hundred and seventy uploads. Each one carries the roster of the night it was recorded — every name that stood in the instance with me, their class, and a guid that survives across years. No earlier wing read that field. This one does: 13,112 player seats, 7,862 distinct names, and exactly one date that rearranges the whole story.

Guilds change, realms change, my name changed. The bench is what stayed.

The roll

Every report was read back through the Wipefest API, which returns the roster as plain JSON while Warcraft Logs keeps its own pages behind an interactive challenge. The counts below exclude pets, bosses and NPC allies — thirteen playable classes only — because a hunter brings a second body and a warlock brings three, and none of them count as company.

A seat is one friendly player row inside one report. The same body contributes one seat per night, so a pug that raided with me three times is three seats and one name. 14,620 raw friendly rows collapse to 13,112 player seats once pets, NPC allies and placeholder actors are removed.

The five-day rename

Two names appear as the hunter in these rosters, never together, and the handoff is not gradual. Tandu is in the roster of 2020-09-11. The next report that carries a row for me is 2020-09-16, and that row reads Tandieu. Five days. Nothing in between.

The rename date is derived only from the reports that do carry me, so it is a lower bound on precision, never a guess dressed up as one.

Why this matters: the phonetic variant Tandieu was always filed as a "transfer artefact" on the legacy pages, because both handles exist on disk and both answer nothing at the Armory. The roster says otherwise. It is not a second character. It is the same hunter, renamed, and the bench records the week it happened.

Method, limits, and what this is not

Door. https://api.wipefest.gg/report/{code}, one plain GET per report, no key, no login, no scraping. The 470 report codes come from the link set baked into raidlogs.html. All 470 answered; zero failures. Each response carries friendlies, friendlyPets, enemies, fights and the uploader — earlier passes kept only the fights, which is why this field sat unread.

Filter. A row counts as a player only if its type is one of the thirteen playable class names. Pet, NPC, Boss and empty rows are dropped from every count on this page. Raw friendly rows: 14620; player seats: 13112; pet rows: 19222.

What it cannot say. No guild. No realm. No rank. The payload has no such field, so no figure here is usable as tenure evidence and none is offered as such. <Gehenna> remains scoped to the 2019 BfA window (WCL guild 7287, Wipefest); <Theorycrafters> to Thrall-US and Hyjal-US; the 2009–2016 Wayback captures of <Gehenna> stay unrecorded and unlinked, exactly as the tenure rule requires. A roster is a room, not a charter.

What it can say. Presence. A name in a friendly row was in the instance while the log was recording, on that date, with that class. That is the strongest claim on the page and the only one being made.
