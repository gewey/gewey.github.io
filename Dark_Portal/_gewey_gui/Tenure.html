Tenure the guilds I am allowed to claim

A guild name is not a membership. Every page in this vault that touches a guild has to pass the same gate: was I actually in it, in that era? This wing is the gate itself — the windows, the receipts, and the material that was deliberately not kept.

I would rather have two admissible captures than twenty-six flattering ones.

Why the exclusions matter. The archive kept a guild alive on paper for years when I was not in it. A capture proves the guild existed on that date. It proves nothing about me. Linking one would turn a true sentence (“this guild was raided in 2011”) into a false one (“I raided in 2011”) without ever writing a lie down.

Method

Rule source. The window itself is arithmetic: the BfA client ran from the 2018-08 prepatch to the Shadowlands prepatch in 2020-10, and the first upload in the vault is 2018-10-01. A guild claim only ever rides inside that band.

Counting source. cdx_captures_total, admissible and excluded come from the tenure_rule object written by the web-ledger pass (2026-11-16) and re-read here, not retyped. Door statuses come from the guild-door payload of the same night. Theorycrafters capture timestamps come from the Wayback CDX payload.

What is never done. No pre-tenure <Gehenna> capture is listed, linked, dated or described — here, on legacy.html, on tandu_legacy.html, or in Tandumonium.agent-index.md. The count of 24 is the only residue, and it exists so the omission is legible instead of looking like an oversight.
