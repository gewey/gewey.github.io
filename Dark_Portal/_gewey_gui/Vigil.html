how a sitting is cut
A new sitting opens on every login row, and also wherever two consecutive events are more than 45 minutes apart — the same rule Clockwork uses, so the two wings cannot disagree about what "a session" means. A reload never splits one: the client reloaded, the person did not leave.

what a duration is
First event to last event in the sitting. The tape samples position every couple of seconds while the client is awake, so the span is honest to within one sample; it is not the /played counter, which lives in Heartbeat and Stride.

what a discovery name is
The tape writes area ids, and sometimes a name. Where it wrote no name, AnimaCards' area:<id> ledger supplies it. 1 row still has neither, and is printed as an id rather than guessed.

what is refused
Quest ids are not linked: every one on this tape is a private-server quest, and Wowhead answers 404 for all of them — a tooltip that 404s is worse than none, so the title the tape cached is printed instead. Item ids are linked, because those are real items (linen cloth, copper ore) and the tooltip resolves them. No player row claims a guild or realm — the tape keeps neither. No sitting is called complete when the tape shows no logout: those rows say no logout and mean it.
