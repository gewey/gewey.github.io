07. Method & Limitswhat a loot row can and cannot prove

what the purse says
  • Linen Cloth is the single most looted item: 377 units.
  • The Barrens is the busiest loot zone, with 764 rows.
  • 1,370 kill rows produced 3,569 loot rows - a 1:2.61 ratio.
  • The addon saved 3,569 loot rows and 15 ledger rows in its own cache.
how it was read
Goldmind writes three tapes: ledger.rows (post/sale, pipe-separated, epoch second per row), loot.rows (K = kill with zone, subzone, creature, level and coords; L = loot with item^qty pairs), and eventlog (AUCTION_* events). Item names are resolved through the addon's own items cache of 6,111 entries; unknown IDs print raw. Copper is rendered as g/s/c.
what it does not claim
Loot rows are bag arrivals, not drops - a vendor purchase looks the same as a corpse. The 15 ledger rows are what the addon chose to keep, not a full auction history. Purchases by other players are invisible. Deposit refunds are not reconciled, so profit per row is gross.
