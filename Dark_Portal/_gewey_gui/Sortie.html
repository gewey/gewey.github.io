The Sorties one card per WoWCombatLog file

A combat log is written once per client launch that had logging enabled. Each file is a single continuous sortie: a dungeon entered, cleared and left behind.

Encounter Ladder every ENCOUNTER_START / ENCOUNTER_END pair

Duration is the client's own measurement. Every boss in this residue died, so bar length is time-to-fell — the long bars are the fights that actually pushed back.

Damage Ledger party members credited by the log

Totals are summed from the log's own amount field. Reflected damage (Fire Shield, thorns) is credited to whoever's shield did the work — which is why a Fire Shield can out-total a sword swing.

Kill Ledger PARTY_KILL, grouped by creature

Intensity log events per clock minute

Each bar is one minute of the run; taller means the client wrote more lines. Trash packs, boss pulls and the quiet walk out all have a shape.

The Crowd every player the log overheard

Combat logging does not filter to your group — it records every player who casts in range, so these two files also caught a city going about its business. Classes are inferred from cast signatures and never stated by the log: treat the chip as a guess with evidence behind it, not a fact.

Method how this was read, and what it cannot say

parserTimeline\sortie_extract.py reads Logs\WoWCombatLog*.txt as CSV, one event per line, using the advanced-logging layout where the amount sits directly after ownerGUID. Output lands in Timeline\data\sortie.json.
read-onlyThe game install is never written. Only the JSON ledger and this page are produced; every other wing is untouched and nothing was deleted.
one log = one sortieFiles are grouped by file, not by session. A log left open across two dungeons would merge into a single card.
counting souls168 is every distinct player name appearing anywhere in either file. Residue.html reports 167 because it registers players from casting events only. Both counts are honest; they answer slightly different questions.
inferenceClass chips come from a curated per-class spell list matched against each player's casts — 57 of 168 souls scored a label. It is evidence, not fact. Bystanders often score nothing and stay unlabelled.
blind spotsNo loot, no XP, no quest text, no gold: combat logs carry none of it. Reward residue lives on the Chronicle and Frontier wings instead.
