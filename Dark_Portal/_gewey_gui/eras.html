How You Date A Ghost

Dead: file timestamps

The oldest file on the whole retail tree is GeweyGUI.tga at 2026-06-06. A 2026 reinstall resets every mtime, and AddOns.txt on all 54 toons shares one bulk-copy stamp (2026-09-16 18:31:44). Timestamps prove copying, not playing.

Alive: macro text

Nobody bulk-copies macros-cache.txt — it is hand-typed, and a macro that references an item from 2016 only exists because someone kept carrying it forward. Macro bodies are the real stratigraphy.

Alive: ghost addons

AddOns.txt remembers addons whose folders are gone — WorldFlightMap is still listed as disabled on all three Tandu toons but has no folder under Interface\AddOns. Ghost rows date the list.

Where the record goes dark. Between the 2008 start and the 2026 reinstall there is no continuous file history: retail WTF lifetime is short, and the old armory snapshots that would have caught each rename are either unarchived or rate-limited. Anything below tagged weak is inference from naming and lore, not proof.

The Layers

Layer I — The Blood Elf 2008 → WotLK era Sin'dorei

  • Realm identity 86-*. The account's character-list-order.txt carries three GUID families — 86- (12 toons), 3661- (28, Hyjal), 3678- (13, Thrall). 86 is the lowest realm id on the account and maps to Shadowsong: the oldest realm identity the account owns.
  • The Tandu folder on Shadowsong still exists — a 0 B macros-cache.txt, AddOns.txt only. A name that survived as a folder after its character data aged out.
  • Guild: <Gehenna>, Borean Tundra / Shadowsong cluster.

Layer II — The Undead Stint Forsaken / Dark Ranger Undead

  • The only race detour in eighteen years — and per the standing rule the character was still a blood elf underneath: a Sin'dorei wearing undeath, not a different person. The Dark Ranger identity survives into the current agent persona (Tinkerer Dark Ranger of Tesseract City).
  • weak No disk artifact names the race transition; this layer is carried by the persona and the legacy ledger, not by a file.

Layer III — Legion 2016 → 2018 fingerprints

  • Pocket Fel Spreader — the Legion pre-patch toy, still welded into the Camouflage macro.
  • Archmage Vargoth's Spare Staff — order-hall furniture, now a /use line next to Feign Death.
  • Order-hall era macros are the first layer where the cache reads like a player instead of a name.

Layer IV — Battle for Azeroth 2018 → 2020 <Gehenna> tenure

  • Mirror of Envious Dreams (8.1.5) and Echoes of Rezan (Zandalar) — two BfA souvenirs in one cache.
  • Warcraft Logs guild id 7287, Uldir / Battle of Dazar'alor window; the 2019 bookmark sheet ported into legacy.html.
  • This is the only era where external raid evidence is even theoretically his — see the tenure rule on Raid Logs.

Layer V — Shadowlands 2020 → 2022 proven by macro

  • The account-wide cache carries a covenant-detection macro: /run local G=GetSpellInfo SetMacroSpell(GetRunningMacro(), G"Resonating Arrow" or G"Flayed Shot" or G"Death Chakram" or G"Wild Spirits") — Kyrian / Necrolord / Venthyr / Night Fae in one line. Nothing else dates a cache to Shadowlands this precisely.
  • Orophea's Lyre (Korthia) sits in the /use stack beside Feign Death.
  • /btwl a Tandu — a BtWLoadouts set named after the old handle, still bound to a key.
  • /console farclip 777, groundEffectDensity 256, maxLOD 3, SkyCloudLOD 3 — the "make the world look like a painting" utility block, written when the machine could not.

Layer VI — Dragonflight 2022 → 2024 thumb on the scale

  • Aerial Halt(skyriding) in the #N- macro — dynamic flight, and the reason the current main is a Dracthyr at all.
  • Muzzle, Coordinated Assault, Survival of the Fittest, Fortitude of the Bear — the Dragonflight talent vocabulary, rebuilt across the whole bar.
  • The macro book is written for three specs at once ([spec:1] BM / [spec:2] MM / [spec:3] SV): one button, three lives.

Layer VII — The War Within 2024 → 2026 on disk

Layer VIII — Midnight 2026 → now Dracthyr

Where The Next Layer Comes From

The one unopened door

Warcraft Logs is the only site that would hold a per-pull record of BfA raiding, and it sits behind a Cloudflare challenge that does not clear under automation. A logged-in human opening guild 7287 → Reports → 2018–2019 would give this page its first real DPS row. Everything else has been swept — see the sweep log. The pre-2018 decade is now measured rather than assumed: Origins runs 33 exact-URL queries and gets 31 empty answers plus a control, which is what an unarchived character sheet looks like from the outside.
