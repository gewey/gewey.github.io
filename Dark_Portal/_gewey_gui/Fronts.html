07. Method & Limitswhy a war log is not a war

what the fronts say
  • The map is split 34 Alliance / 33 Horde - a one-front margin.
  • Lesi Bear Cave holds the most outposts, with 2 capture records.
  • Campaign 20,261,110 is logged for THE HORDE.
  • 11 killing-blow entries and 15 victory events are cached.
how it was read
OverlordDB keeps zones (front key, owner, status, capturedTime, holdTimeRequired), outposts (site, owner guild and faction, held capturer, claim time), outpostCaptureCounts (site:pool:guild with a capture count and last capturer), leaderboardDisplayCache.byFaction and dominationVictoryEvents.byPool.pve. Front keys are humanised for display; no ownership is inferred.
what it does not claim
This is the addon's synthetic campaign, not live realm state: the client only sees what it is told and only writes on logout. Campaign kills read 0 at the moment of capture, so an empty scoreboard is a pause, not a peace. Leaderboard counts are pve-pool killing blows, not honour.
