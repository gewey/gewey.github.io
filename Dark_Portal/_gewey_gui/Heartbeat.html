Method & Limitshow this page was grown, and what it refuses to claim

what the pulse says
  • 43 launches between 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-15 21:22 — 51h 35m of client wall time.
  • The GPU reported 11 times: floor 43°C, peak 57°C, 32.3% VRAM.
  • 6.0 GB of assets across 52 loading screens this capture.
  • Login to world socket: 26.00s, 0 non-OK codes.
  • The played clock read 37h 29m at 2026-11-15 21:21.
  • 274 screenshots sit beside these logs (228.8 MB).
how it was read
A heartbeat is an Opened for Append header paired with the WowMoviePlayer Shutdown that follows it in Movie.log; the first block predates the header and starts at its first line, flagged inferred. Handshake offsets are measured from the first line of Connection.log. GPU samples are the Periodic Gpu Status Report rows verbatim. Loading throughput is the client's own Files/Bytes/MB/s self-report. Nothing is written outside Timeline\data\heartbeat.json and this page; the prior render is copied to _gewey_gui\.history\ first, never deleted.
what it does not claim
A launch is not a session in the Timeline sense — the client can idle on the login or character screen for the whole block. Movie.log rotates, so launches before the current file are invisible; the earliest block has no header and its length is approximate. Log timestamps carry no year or zone; the year is assumed 2026 and all times are local. GPU reports are periodic samples, not peaks. The played clock is polled by an addon, so a quiet capture can hold no samples at all.
