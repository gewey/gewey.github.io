Method & Limitshow every number above was obtained

Read-only. Nothing in Z:\World of Warcraft\_forever_ was written, moved or deleted. Every parser opens files for reading and nothing else.

One implementation. Quest envelopes, progression checkpoints, GPU samples and boot marks come from frontier_extract.py, imported rather than copied, so this wing and the Frontier wing cannot disagree about the same file.

Timestamps. Client logs carry no year; the year is inferred from the file's mtime. Epoch values (Raider.IO checkpoints, Goldmind, WoWTaskManager) are rendered in this machine's local time. Where a fact is bracketed between two checkpoints, the card says so instead of picking a second.

What is estimated. Exactly one figure: the step count (317.7 yards), which CountSteps derives from movement. Copper, kills, deaths, quests, xp and played time are read straight out of the addon that recorded them.

Regenerate. python "Z:\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Dark_Portal\Timeline\chronicle_extract.py" — the prior render is archived to _gewey_gui\.history\ first.
