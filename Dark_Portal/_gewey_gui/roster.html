Method & Limits

  • Source: Z:\World of Warcraft\_retail_\WTF\Account\QUAZZARR\<Realm>\<Character>\ — read-only, never modified.
  • Files: every regular file in the character folder, recursive, including SavedVariables\.
  • Macros: counted from ^VER 3 <id> "<name>" records in macros-cache.txt.
  • Addons: non-comment lines in AddOns.txt, enabled and disabled alike.
  • Dates: filesystem mtime — when a file was last touched, not when the character was created. A client reinstall resets them all at once, which is exactly why 45 shells share one date.
  • What this cannot tell you: playtime, /played, item level, or anything the client keeps server-side. Those live in the Armory and Raider.IO, not in WTF — see Character and Gear.
