Method & Limitshow this page was grown, and what it refuses to claim

what the residue says
  • Fastest boss: Taragaman the Hungerer at 17s; longest: Lord Pythas at 44s.
  • Tandumonium-Ebbeson-Normal-US holds the blade record with 73 killing blows.
  • Duggywanna-Tuggy-Normal-US tops the damage ledger at 2.43M over 4,538 landed events.
  • Busiest capture: 2026-11-15 with 41,017 combat-log lines.
  • 63.1k lines collapse to 41 event kinds — auras and damage ticks outnumber decisions.
how it was read
Every WoWCombatLog*.txt under Logs\ is re-read start to finish. The timestamp prefix is stripped, then each CSV row is parsed with Python's csv module so quoted names survive. Damage uses the amount field after the advanced-log info GUID (swing rows: 11, spell rows: 14); healing uses field 14. Screenshots are inventoried by filename timestamp, QuestCache by its id=/r= lines, macros by VER 3 headers, SavedVariables by size. Nothing is written outside Timeline\data\residue.json and this page; the prior render is copied to _gewey_gui\.history\ first, never deleted.
what it does not claim
UNIT_DIED repeats corpse GUIDs, so creature deaths are not a kill count — PARTY_KILL is. Damage totals are summed recorded hits, not DPS, and omit unlogged absorbs. Healing is undercounted because several heal rows in this build carry a shorter field layout. Screenshot dates come from filenames, not EXIF. The logs cover only the sessions the client captured; quiet weeks are invisible.
