Method. Generated, not hand-typed. itinerary_extract.py tokenises three SavedVariables with the same Lua reader almanac_extract.py uses, then derives every figure above from them. Three rules kept the numbers honest: zone names come only from Saga's zone_id to zone_name pairs, so no map id is guessed; killer names are matched to deaths on (zone, level) because the two addons disagree on the total (Travelogue 28 against Saga 28); and quests are reported by value rather than title because Saga stores only a quest_id. Distances are the addon's own yards, unconverted. /played (42h 13m) and the tracked clock (19h 42m) measure different things and are both printed as such.
