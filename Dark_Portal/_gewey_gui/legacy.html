The honest limits

Retail is a reinstall, not a continuous 2008 install: the oldest file on the tree is 2026-08-14. There is no local Wrath/Cata/MoP combat log to find. The 2008–2016 record is only on the internet — which is exactly why the Paper Trail section above is load-bearing.

Warcraft Logs and WoWProgress both sit behind Cloudflare and refuse scripted reads. They have to be opened by a human in a browser. That is a wall, not a bug.
