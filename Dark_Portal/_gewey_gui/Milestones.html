method

Read-only. Every row is folded from a payload a sibling wing already wrote, de-duplicated on (kind, timestamp, title), then sorted. No log is re-parsed here, so a re-run produces the same tape as long as the inputs are unchanged.

limits

Saga and Travelogue store a quest id and never a title, so turn-ins are counted and timed, not named. Achievements carry a date only. Realm-side quest names, item names and NPC loot tables are not in these files and are not invented here.
