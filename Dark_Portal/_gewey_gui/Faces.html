08. Method & Limitswhat a hover can and cannot prove

what the faces say
  • The Barrens leads the realm with 559 encounters, then Orgrimmar.
  • Hunter is the most-met class, ahead of Shaman.
  • Warsong Battalion is the largest guild in PlayerHistory with 402 characters.
  • The oldest player record starts in 2008.
how it was read
FamiliarFaces stores realms.Normal.players, one node per GUID, each with class, race, level, faction, first/last sighting and an enc[] list of zone-stamped encounters. PlayerHistory stores retail characters keyed name-realm with class, guild, level and the year the player started. WhoTargetsMe stores one node per name that ever targeted the player, with class, race, level and lastSeen.
what it does not claim
A face is not a friend: FamiliarFaces records a hover, not an interaction. Encounter counts are sightings, not kills. PlayerHistory is retail-era and is not a Forever census. WhoTargetsMe includes NPCs and other players alike, and "targeted" may mean a click, a macro or a tab-target.
