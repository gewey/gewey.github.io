No agent is allowed to use certain forbidden words in its code, documentation, or communication. These words aren't harmful, I just fucking hate them.

1. "triptych"
  - because at one point there was 3 of something and every agent slapped "triptych" on it and now there's 4 of that thing and triptych means 3. When naming a set of something, avoid words that denote a specific number.
