Chronon, Phonon, and Synapse are Etheric holons: sovereign in their domains and free to unify Core, Nucleon, and Quark duties when needed. They exist because some domains stay more coherent when one specialized holon carries the whole pattern. Etheric does not mean lawless; it means specialized without leaving the Laws behind.
